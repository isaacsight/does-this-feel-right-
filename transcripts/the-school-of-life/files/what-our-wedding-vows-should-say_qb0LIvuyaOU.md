In the interest of a saner, more realistic and more practical attitude to marriage, here are some suggestions for new kinds of marriage vows. We should good-naturedly admit that the person we're marrying is very far from perfect, and articulate all the ways in which they will prove irritating, difficult, sometimes irrational, and often unable to sympathize or understand us.
