At The School of Life, we think differently. We believe that good friendships follow certain principles which can be identified, discussed, and taught. We firmly believe that we can learn to become better friends and are not any of us born knowing how to be. Here then are some of the principles of being a good friend. Firstly, asking the right questions. We need to learn the art of teasing out of people the bits of themselves that are sincere, vulnerable,
