Flaubert couldn't stand newspapers.  He belonged to a generation that had experienced the rise of mass circulation media at first hand and he believed that this was spreading a new kind of stupidity which he termed  'la bêtise' (stupidity) into every corner of France. Idiocy wasn't the same as ignorance for Flaubert because it was compatible with knowing a lot of things it just meant understanding nothing.
