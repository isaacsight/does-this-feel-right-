A functioning economy has to have a healthy level of demand in it for lack of demand invariably leads to the most awful specter of all: recession. Therefore, governments carefully track how much demand there is and are careful to apply a stimulus whenever it looks as if there isn't any. This involves maneuvers like lowering interest rates, cutting taxes, and loosening credit in all manner of ways. For example, in a typical piece of demand stimulus in December 2008, fearing a recession, the Australian government gave a present of $1,000 to every family in the country, encouraging them to go out and spend so as to avoid economic disaster. Currently, the focus of economics is entirely on the quantity of demand, on trying to get people to spend more money on, well, pretty much anything.
