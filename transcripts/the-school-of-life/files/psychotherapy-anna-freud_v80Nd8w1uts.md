Perhaps most importantly for us, she is our finest guide to what we call DEFENCE MECHANISMS which she described best in her 1936 book The Ego and Mechanisms of Defence. The book laid out for the first time the core idea that we instinctively try to protect our ‘ego’ (our acceptable picture of who we are) with a variety of defences.
