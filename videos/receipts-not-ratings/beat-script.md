# RECEIPTS, NOT RATINGS — beat script
kernel.chat ISSUE 414 · YouTube · 16:9 · ~78s · NO voiceover · music + type only

## Concept angle (one sentence)
The film is a paper trail being stamped — every truth arrives as print
settling onto ivory stock, and the one faked number (the 2 that became
a 5) is the only element that ever moves violently; the receipt is the
world, the rating is the intruder.

## Identity (from kernel.chat design system — brand truth)
- Ground: ivory `#FAF9F6` (light canvas — cinematic light, not dark)
- Ink: `#1F1E1D`
- Accent: brick `#9E3A2B` (dispatch "record-of-record" default), ONE hue
- Serif: EB Garamond (embedded 400/700) — headlines, doctrine
- Mono: Space Mono (embedded 400/700) — slugs, datelines, metadata chrome
- CJK: Noto Sans JP (embedded 400/700) — Japanese sublines
- Texture: paper grain, hairline rules, registration marks, folio numbers
- Grammar: dispatch — wire slugs, datelines, stamps, overlines

## Rhythm (named before implementing)
`type-type-SLAM | steady | print-rhythm | SLOW…SLAM(climax) | connective | doctrine-hold | outro-fade`

Primary transition: **vertical push** (page/wire feed, editorial).
Accent: hard cuts on percussive hits. Climax gets the boldest treatment.
Outro: color-dip/fade (only final scene may exit).

## Beats

### B1 · COLD OPEN — the wire wakes up (0.0–9.0s)
World: a wire terminal on paper. Mono slug TYPES ON across the top:
`KERNEL.CHAT WIRE · 414 · VII·26 · DISPATCH`. A hairline rule DRAWS.
Then the masthead SLAMS in three hits on the beat:
  "Receipts," (slam, scale+blur) / "Not" (side snap, brick) / "Ratings." (rise+rotate)
Subline SETTLES: 評価ではなく証跡を (Noto Sans JP)
Chrome: folio `001/006`, registration marks in corners, dateline strip.
→ vertical push

### B2 · FIELD — the constraint kept moving (9.0–21.0s)
Overline: `01 · FIELD`. Three mono words step on the beat:
  TOKEN → struck through. CPU → struck through. ATTENTION → holds, brick underline sweeps.
On-screen line (Garamond, large): "The constraint kept moving."
Support (mono, small): "what you really want to optimize is how often
you need to be in the loop" — attributed `— P. STEINBERGER, ON THE RECORD`
→ vertical push

### B3 · RECEIPTS — attach the transcript (21.0–34.0s)
World: a receipt PRINTS line by line (mono rows FILL downward like a
till receipt, quick per-line steps). Headline: "Attach the transcript."
Quote line lands: "the longer your transcript is, the higher my
confidence that you actually care."
Chrome: perforation dots, `TRANSCRIPT ATTACHED` stamp small in brick.
→ hard cut (percussive) into the climax

### B4 · CAUTION — the two that became a five (34.0–50.0s) ★ CLIMAX
The slowest, then the hardest hit in the film.
Stage near-empty: giant ink "2" alone on ivory (long hold, music thins).
Mono caption types: "an automated pass rated the PR…"
Then a violent strike: the 2 is CROSSED OUT; a brick "5" STAMPS over it
(scale 1.6→1, blur, paper-shake); the word "merge" types after it, lowercase.
Caption: "…and someone typed over it with a five."
Beat of silence. Small ink line: "A number invites a number-shaped lie."
→ vertical push (energy resets)

### B5 · LOOP — send it back where it started (50.0–60.0s)
World: the review loop as a wire route. A brick dot TRAVELS a drawn
path that folds back to its origin (svg path draw). Headline:
"Send it back where it started." Support (mono): "the originating
session holds the context — write the decision into the PR."
→ vertical push

### B6 · DOCTRINE — the two sentences (60.0–70.0s)
Near-empty ivory. Two lines, Garamond, large, set apart:
  "A rating is a claim."   (ink)
  "A receipt is a trace."  (brick emphasis on "trace")
Then small mono: "receipts do not replace judgment — they make it
cheaper to apply and expensive to fake around."
→ gentle crossfade

### B7 · COLOPHON — end of dispatch (70.0–78.0s)
The seal: brick circle stamp `RECEIPTS OVER RATINGS · VII·26` STAMPS in.
Wordmark: KERNEL.CHAT (Garamond 700) + rule + `VII·26`.
Mono terminator line: `END OF DISPATCH · KERNEL.CHAT/414 · INK STILL WET`
Final scene exception: fade to ivory (not black — house is paper).

## Audio
- BGM: sparse, percussive, editorial — brushed drums / muted piano pulse,

- SFX: typewriter keys (B1, B4 captions), stamp thunk (B4 five, B7 seal),
  paper slide (transitions), receipt-printer tick (B3).
- NO voiceover.
