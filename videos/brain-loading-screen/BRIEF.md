## Intent

A four-to-six-minute behavioral-science explainer built around the 2025
Scientific Reports paper “Pause before action.” The narration should be
accurate, conversational, and comedic: serious scientific language colliding
with ordinary mistakes such as sending an email too quickly. The viewer should
leave understanding that a brief forced pause improved accuracy in the study’s
visual judgment task, while a longer pause increased irritation, and that this
does not establish a universal one-second rule for every decision.
