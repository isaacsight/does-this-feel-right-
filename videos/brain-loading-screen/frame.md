## Overview

The video is an illustrated institutional comedy: an old government office
inside the mind treats ordinary decisions like matters of state.

## The frame

Illustrations occupy the full canvas and remain the hero. Graphic additions are
scarce: opening title, scientific terms, one result statement, the caveat, and
the closing phrase. They sit on opaque parchment or ink fields so they never
compete with detailed scenery.

## Composition rules

- Preserve the hand-inked sepia illustration as the dominant visual layer.
- Cobalt blue always represents thought or evidence.
- Tomato red marks exactly one crisis, control, or official action.
- Use hard cuts for jokes and short dissolves for scientific section changes.
- Camera movement stays between three and six percent except for directed crop
  traversals inside wide explanatory images.
- Stillness is deliberate on “Wait one second,” “No,” and final reaction holds.
- Captions avoid the lower-center action when necessary; use upper or side
  placement scene by scene.

## Typography

Archivo Black supplies only short declarative titles. Space Mono is the
institutional voice for captions, scientific terms, and measured values. Never
place long paragraphs on screen.

## Avoid

- Rounded interface containers, web-style cards, glossy 3D, gradients, neon,
  soft drop shadows, generated labels, or decorative text.
- More than one full-saturation red element in a frame.
- Camera moves that call attention to themselves.
