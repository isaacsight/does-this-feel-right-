## Video direction

The edit treats each illustration as a miniature theatrical set. Camera movement
is restrained: three-to-six-percent pushes, lateral pans that reveal cause and
effect, and stillness on verbal punchlines. Hard cuts introduce jokes; gentle
dissolves separate scientific sections. Cobalt fabric guides the eye between
evidence and action. Tomato red always identifies the current crisis or control.
