# CLAIMS — The Lonely Chapter (episode 5, working title)

Topic chosen 2026-08-10, riding Modern Wisdom's live coinage (Chris Williamson,
"the lonely chapter" — the stretch after you outgrow one circle and before you
find the next; #1130 aired 2026-08-01). Verified rows only; every gag must be
one of these facts.

## May NOT say

- "Loneliness kills like smoking" stated flat — always the effect-size framing
  (row 3), and the film may note the claim has been telephone-gamed.
- No invented percentages, no "all men," no epidemic language beyond the
  Surgeon General's own word ("epidemic" IS his word, 2023 — quotable).
- The lodges are not mocked as silly; the fezzes are funny, the PRODUCT was
  real. The joke is that we had industrial-scale friendship infrastructure
  and dismantled it.
- Nothing about the narrator; the narrator never becomes a persona.

## The spine (provisional, for the room)

The man's want, smaller than ever: he wants to call his friend. Not make a
friend — call one he already has. The phone weighs six hundred pounds.
Meanwhile history sits behind him: his great-grandfather had a temple, a
costume, a Tuesday. The science act prices what he's lost in hours and
mortality, played straight. The mercy: the 200 hours are not a debt, they're
a recipe — and "we should hang out sometime" finally becomes a date on a
calendar. It was never about the lodge. It was about Tuesday.
