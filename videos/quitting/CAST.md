# CAST — Quitting

Counts are LAW at point of use. No character is a likeness of any real
person; the gym-wall author and the register's namesake are never drawn.

## Principals

**THE MAN** — the recurring protagonist: a small slight man with a round face
and a big confident nose, his head bald and smooth except for a single lonely
PAIR of hairs at the very top of his crown — one thin hair kinking left, one
leaning right — a flat VERMILION RED coat with THREE round buttons, thin
stick legs, ABSURDLY oversized shoes. WANT: to put something down. HIS HANDS
are the film's subject: CLOSED around objects until the final act, then OPEN.
NEVER smug.

**THE WALL-KEEPER** — a broad gym coach type in a SAGE GREEN tracksuit with
ONE whistle on ONE lanyard, arms folded, monumentally still — the rule's
weather, not a villain; he simply stands where the wall stands.

**THE WALL** — the monument object (the catalog's fourth): a green-painted
gym wall carrying ONE huge completely BLANK framed plaque (the rule lives in
narration; the plaque never letters). Portable: it appears behind the man in
the kitchen, at the theatre, in the field — the rule follows him.

**THE THREE PAPERS** — ONE blank lease sheet, ONE completely BLANK ticket,
ONE blank schedule grid — the objects the closed hands hold.

## Supporting

**THE DONKEY** — one grey donkey with a composed patient face; ONE neat hay
bale; ONE full pail. Deadpan dignity; in the club act it stands in the
spotlight circle before the silhouettes.
**THE SCALE** — one brass grocery scale with ONE thumb resting on its pan.
**THE TICKET QUEUE** — FIVE coated figures in driving snow, formally still.
**THE TWO FLOORS (straight act)** — elderly residents with composed dignified
faces; ONE small potted plant; ONE watering can; TWO mirrored corridor/room
stagings (the floor that decides, warm; the floor that is handled, handled).
**THE PARTY** — a banquet room, balloons, ONE completely BLANK banner, cake.
**CLUB SILHOUETTES** — rows of individuated dark figures, always present in
any club frame.

## Environments (11 — gym/wall frames ≤22%)

1. the gym (wall, plaque, wall-keeper)
2. the kitchen table (the three papers)
3. the shop (unlit sign BLANK, the lease's home)
4. the banquet hall (the party act)
5. the scale room (flat cream field with the brass scale — void-adjacent)
6. the snowstorm street + theatre entrance
7. the field (the donkey act; also its CLUB setpiece version)
8. the chemistry room (hexagon-free BLANK textbook pages, ashtray)
9. the nursing home, two floors (straight act — warm plain daylight)
10. the front step, snow, dusk (the ending)
11. void (flat cream, connective)

## Palette / era

Vermilion = the Man. Sage = the wall-keeper + gym. Ink blue = snow dusk +
club dark. Mustard = lamplight, cake, the plant's pot. Dusty orange = the
theatre + banquet warmth. The straight act keys warm cream.

## Interaction quota

The Man's hands appear CLOSED around an object in at least SIX frames before
the step; the ending stages: lays the ticket down → hands OPEN → the plant
lifted to the sill. The wall-keeper and the Man share at least TWO frames
(the hand-on-shoulder staging: the keeper's hand resting on the Man's
shoulder, both perfectly still). Deadpan crowds are individuated and
composed — never blank-faced; composed-amid-absurdity is a face ACTION.
