## The spine (provisional, for the room)

The unwritten rule carved on every gym wall: never quit. Nobody audits it.
The man's want, tiny as ever: he wants to put something down — the lease,
the league, the law degree, the ticket — and the rule's hand is on his
shoulder. The pattern act: we throw a party for every start, and every start
required a quit that nobody celebrated; starting and quitting are the same
door photographed from opposite sides. The science act prices the grip:
losses loom 2.25 to 1, and the ticket you already paid for marches you
through a snowstorm to a show you don't want to see. The philosophy act: the
donkey. The straight act: the nursing home — the one quit that kills is
quitting the deciding. The mercy: quitting the right thing IS the deciding;
the door out of one room is the door into the next, and it was never about
the door. It was about who's holding the handle.
