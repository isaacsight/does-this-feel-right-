# Short-form extracts — THE EXPLANATION ARRIVES SECOND

Three cuts, 30–45s, taken from the master rather than shot separately. Built
with `tools/shorts/cut-verticals.py` against a `shorts.json` in this directory,
exactly as the polyvagal set was.

Geometry per `docs/video/SHORTS-TEMPLATE.md`: 16:9 letterboxed into the picture
band at y=380, kicker at y=190, captions bottom-anchored 560px up, wrapped on
**measured** width. Safe box centre is x=500, not 540.

---

## 01 — THREE ROOMS · ~40s · Act II

**Kicker:** SAME HEART, THREE ROOMS
**In/out:** "Let me show you the same heart three times" → "Three completely
different feelings."

The strongest standalone. Self-contained, needs no setup, and the payoff is
visual — the three identical bodies with three different faces. Frames i08–i12.

> **Caption copy:** A date, a dark car park, and a start line. Identical heart
> rate. Three different feelings. #interoception #psychology #behavioralscience

**Why it leads:** it is the film's thesis in one image and it works muted.

---

## 02 — THE EVIDENCE IS ONE CARD · ~35s · Act V

**Kicker:** THE VERDICT IS INSTANT
**In/out:** "Sit still and try to count your own heartbeats" → "it arrives with
no doubt in it at all."

The comic mechanism at its clearest: hero failing to count their own pulse,
then the office reaching a total verdict off a single blank card. Frames
i23–i28.

> **Caption copy:** Most people can't count their own heartbeat. Your brain
> reaches a verdict anyway. #interoception #anxiety #howthebrainworks

**Risk note:** depends on i25/i28 landing wordless. If the stamp frame renders
a glyph, this short cannot ship — it is the one extract that hangs entirely on
office frames.

---

## 03 — THE HONEST PART · ~45s · Act VII into VIII

**Kicker:** IT DIDN'T REPLICATE
**In/out:** "The famous experiment here is from nineteen sixty-two" → "It's the
caption underneath it that's a draft."

The one that isn't a joke. Runs the concession — famous study, failed
replication, no clean bodily signature — straight into the useful conclusion.
Frames i36–i43, i50.

> **Caption copy:** The famous experiment behind this idea didn't replicate.
> Here's the part that survived. #science #replicationcrisis #psychology

**Why include a serious cut:** the strand's credibility is the product. A short
that concedes something is the one most likely to be trusted, and it reaches a
different audience than the gag cuts. It will underperform on views. Post it
anyway.

## Ordering

Post **01** first and give it a week. **02** second — it converts best to
people who already met the character. **03** last, or on the channel rather
than to the algorithm.
