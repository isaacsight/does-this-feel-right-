### The one FAIL, and why it matters more than it looks

`m04` is the anchor reveal: the same kitchen with the curtains gone. Its whole
job is that **only the curtains change**. Instead the protagonist is larger,
shifted left, cropped at the knees, and wearing a different expression. A viewer
cannot read "the curtains were never there" if the shot has also moved. m03 and
m05 share the drift, so the cleanest fix is to regenerate m03–m05 as a locked
group against m01, using the in-scene camera language that fixed m38 — the
generic LOCKED_CAMERA clause was not applied to this group at all, because none
of these frames had a previous-in-group reference at generation time.

Cost to fix the FAIL plus the two clear defects (m04, m12, m25): **4 frames,
$0.60.** m10, m30, m31 and m35 are judgement calls for you rather than defects.
