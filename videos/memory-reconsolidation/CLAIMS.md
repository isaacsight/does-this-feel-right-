# CLAIMS TABLE — Your Memory Is Not the Original

**Gate 1 artifact.** Binds narration to evidence. Every factual sentence in
`SCRIPT.md` must map to a row here. Verification at Gate 5 re-checks the
recorded narration against this table — a qualification silently lost in an
edit is treated as a defect, not a style change.

**Tier** is the evidence level from `RESEARCH.md`. **Register** is the
grammatical mood the narration is permitted to use for that claim, and it is
not negotiable during writing.

| Register | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Indicative** | State as fact. "Memory is reconstructive." |
| **Hedged** | State with a named condition. "Under some experimental conditions…" |
| **Attributed** | Say whose claim it is. "One proposed mechanism…" |
| **Contested** | Say out loud that it is disputed, and by whom |

## Prohibited sentences

Hard fails. If any appears in a draft, the draft is wrong.

| Never say | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| "Every time you remember something you change it" | Overstates C8, assumes C11 | "Under some conditions, recalling something appears to leave it open to change" |
| "Your brain rewrites the memory" | Assumes Tier 4 mechanism | "The recalled version may be the one that gets stored" |
| "Memory reconsolidation shows that…" | Presents C11 as established | "Reconsolidation is one proposed explanation, and it is disputed" |
| "Vivid memories are false" | Unsupported and harmful | Nothing — cut the idea |
| "Traumatic memories are unreliable" | Unsupported and harmful | Nothing — cut the idea |
| "Eyewitness testimony is worthless" | Overstates C3–C5 | "Nobody is immune to it" |
| "Therapy can erase a memory" | Irresponsible | Not addressed in this film |
| "Scientists have proved memory is rewritten on recall" | False | "This is an argument that is still running" |
| Any percentage for false-memory implantation | C15 figures are disputed | "Harder than people assume. Not impossible." |

## Where each tier lives in the film

The evidence weakens as the film proceeds, deliberately.

| Section | Tier | Register |
|---|---|---|
| Kitchen / blue curtains | 1 | Indicative |
| Not playback | 1 | Indicative |
| Reveal the archive | 1 | Indicative — the metaphor is for Tier 1, so it is *earned* |
| Later information enters | 2 | Indicative |
| The family story | 1+2 | Hedged |
| What researchers mean by reconsolidation | 3 → 4 | Attributed |
| Evidence section | 4 | **Contested** |
| Ordinary life | 2 | Indicative — deliberately drops back to solid ground |
| ORIGINAL → CURRENT VERSION | — | Editorial |

**Design consequence worth stating plainly:** the archive metaphor is
scientifically licensed by **Tier 1**, which is settled. It does not depend on
reconsolidation being true. That is why the film can spend four minutes on the
archive and then concede Tier 4 without collapsing — the joke was never
resting on the contested claim. If any archive gag *only* makes sense if
reconsolidation is real, that gag is cut.
