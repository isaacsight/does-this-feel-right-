## What was wrong for three drafts

My frames were **static tableaux**: a person standing in a room where something
mildly incorrect is happening, described dryly. Tasteful. Tasteful is not funny.

The register is **the frozen peak of a catastrophe** — the single instant where
physics is at maximum and everyone except one person is being destroyed by it.

## The formula, verbatim in every prompt

Freeze at the exact instant the catastrophe peaks. **One clear cause-and-effect
path** through the image, starting from one small mistake.

**Mechanics:** extreme squash and stretch · curved spines, strong lines of action ·
elongated limbs in flight · accordion-compressed bodies on impact · large readable
faces · purposeful curved motion lines · ONE bold impact burst · a few vibration
marks and compact dust clouds · dramatic foreground scale · slight Dutch angle ·
clear silhouettes · simplified background · one dominant gag plus one smaller
callback.

**Comedy law:** every absurd consequence follows logically from the original
mistake. Chaos is enormous while **at least one character stays completely calm
and professionally serious**. Characters commit to the absurd solution rather
than acknowledging anything is wrong. **The protagonist stays perfectly untouched
and composed** while everyone else suffers impossible physics.

**Must read at thumbnail size.**

**Avoid:** random clutter · restrained editorial poses · realistic physics ·
evenly weighted jokes · excessive flying papers · photorealism · 3D · text,
letters, labels, speech balloons, logos, watermarks.

## Red rule — amended

Previously "one red object, never on a person". In this register the **impact
burst** also wants to be red and it earns it. New rule:

> **One red object, PLUS the impact burst.** Still never on skin, hair or
> clothing. If a frame has no burst, it gets one red object or none.

The validation frame carries three reds (burst, stamp, cake band). Two is the
ceiling — burst plus one object.

## Consequence for the rebuild

Every gag in `SCRIPT-V3.md` must be restaged as a **peak-catastrophe** frame, not
a tableau. Derek's mistakes now have blast radius:

- Short wall → Derek adds a second short wall **and the whole set concertinas**,
  launching two archivists, while Derek calmly measures the new wall
- PROBABLY box → Derek pulls out the wrong face; **the memory it lands on
  detonates**, and Derek is already reaching in for another
- "Smashed" → the word is a physical blow; the protagonist's head snaps back,
  glass sprays, **and Derek files the glass while still airborne**
- Copy becomes master → Derek bins the original **as the archive collapses
  behind him**, unbothered

Three wide shots in the whole film. Everything else is close, with foreground
scale.
