**Physics is under target and that is deliberate.** The briefed +25% silliness
is delivered by the *archive as a place* — 36% of frames — rather than by
cartoon violence. Making bodies deflate in a film about the unreliability of
recollection would compete with the mechanism; making a workshop visibly
improvise **is** the mechanism. The gate question is unchanged: *can the viewer
be wrong about what they're seeing?* If yes, literal.
