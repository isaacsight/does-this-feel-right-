# Why Humans Need Rituals

## Intent

Create a funny, compassionate science explainer that begins with one
embarrassingly specific pre-performance ritual, moves through anthropology and
experimental psychology, and returns to ordinary life. The video should make
rituals understandable without treating religious traditions as curiosities or
claiming that ritual is magic.

The recurring nameless character represents a human being facing a threshold.
The narrator never needs to name or directly describe the character. The
character silently carries the emotional story through posture, facial
expression, and action.
