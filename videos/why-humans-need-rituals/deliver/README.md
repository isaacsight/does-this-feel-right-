**Title**
> Why Humans Need Rituals

**Alternates** — The Psychology of Small Ceremonies · Why We Tap Tables Before Big Moments · What Rituals Actually Do to Your Brain

**Description**

> We tap tables twice, straighten objects already straight, and tie our left shoes with moral seriousness. We know the shoe doesn't control the job interview—yet an interruption leaves us strangely exposed, as if the future caught us without its paperwork.
>
> This film explores the psychology and anthropology of ritual: why predictable action calms an uncertain mind, how Arnold van Gennep’s "difficult middle" structures life transitions, and why moving together turns a crowd into a "we."
>
> Sources & Research:
> - Van Gennep, A. (1909), "Les rites de passage"
