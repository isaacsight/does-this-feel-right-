## Video direction

The red-jacket lead travels through one connected outdoor threshold world.
Every plate is a late-1980s four-ink risograph manga print using exactly black,
cream paper, tomato red, moss green, and cyan. Tomato red is the only accent.
Animals, robots, and fictional beings share the lead’s facial and body-language
system.

There are ninety-six unique stills, four per frame. Each still carries one
narration clause for roughly four seconds. The edit uses hard cuts only. No
pans, zooms, parallax, animation, captions, music, sound effects, lettering,
logos, or repeated images. Every prompt must pass
`../../docs/image-prompt-law.md`; every result must pass its anomaly gate.
