## The direction: DEADPAN GEOMETRY

> The gap between people is the subject of every frame.

Blocking is the comedy. Compositions squared and formal, TWO figures held
far apart in generous flat space, the empty middle of the frame doing the
talking. The avoidance arc a walker carves around an acquaintance is staged
as visible geometry — a curved line of footprints in snow, a wide detour
around a lamppost — never as a diagram, never as drawn dotted lines.
Characters hold perfectly still mid-avoidance with completely composed
faces. Nothing dramatic in the light; the drama is the distance.

Accents:

- **THE PLATFORM** is this film's monument (the 1972 act's home): one long
  commuter platform, formally symmetrical, the same FIVE coated commuters
  standing in the same five positions whenever it recurs — the recurrence IS
  the joke and the claim.
- **CLUB** for ONE frame only (the rationed twist beat): dark room, ONE hard
  spotlight circle, populated silhouette surround (paid theatre lesson),
  deep warm brick-red back wall.
- **WARM ACT** (the barista act): plain warm daylight, full Pullman gravity,
  no geometry gags — the one room where the distance closes.

**Named failure classes to gate:** empty frames with NO cast read as flat
(flat-run laws v1+v2 both active); two-figure frames must state "exactly TWO
figures" at point of use or the occupants prior grows a crowd; composed ≠
blank — composed-amid-avoidance is a face ACTION.

## The rule this episode is built on

> The avoider is us. The grip on silence is priced, never mocked, and the
> film never says hello on our behalf — it prices the hello and stands back.

## The locked clause

> a MID-CENTURY ANIMATION BACKGROUND AND CEL in the American limited-animation
> idiom of the late 1950s: flat gouache-painted backgrounds and flat painted
> cel figures with confident tapering ink outlines and no interior shading, in
> a warm graphic palette of INK BLUE, mustard GOLD, sage GREEN, dusty ORANGE,
> cream and flat VERMILION RED, each scene keying its own dominant colour from
> that set. Expressions are BOLD, exaggerated and readable at a glance. Visible
> gouache texture, flat matte, NO gradients, NO airbrushed shading, NO
> photographic texture, NO three-dimensional rendering.

Plus standing laws: no text of any kind, blank signs/newspapers/cups, no
franchise designs, no likeness of any real person (no frame may resemble
Larry David, Jimmy Carr, Epley, or Milgram), one coherent scene, 16:9.
