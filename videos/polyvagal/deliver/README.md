## Known defects — decide before or after posting

Three items from the frame-by-frame review. None of them block a publish;
all three are worth fixing if the film gets re-cut.

1. **`w01` at 3:49 renders the word CLATTER twice.** Text baked into a
   generated frame — the failure mode we fought all film. Reshoot.
2. **`v17` at 1:13 shows an empty chair** on "And here's the third." The
   third state needs the person limp *in* the chair; empty reads as "they
   left," which inverts the point and breaks the three-chair anchor.
3. **Four holds run past the 8s ceiling** — v03 (11.6s), v41 (10.1s),
   w02 (9.9s), and v09/v48 at 8.0s. A re-time pass would break them up.

Reviewed from contact sheets, not in motion. Defects that only show in
motion may remain.
