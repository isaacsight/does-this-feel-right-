# THE THIRD THING — frame book

72 frames. 16:9. Reference-conditioned hero via `nano-banana-2/edit`,
`params.image_urls` nested inside `params`.

## The four rules, applied

1. **Information AND a punchline.** Each row below names both. If a frame's
   *punchline* column is empty at the gate, the frame is rewritten, not shipped.
2. **Overexaggerate.** The hero is ruined, not inconvenienced. One stage past
   plausible, played dead straight.
3. **Continuity.** ONE WORLD: the hero's flat (grey sofa, wall clock), one
   office (long table), one café. Recurring props: **a red mug**, the wall
   clock, the same grey sofa. The mug appears in Acts I, IV and VII.
4. **Situations, not symbols.** No diagrams with a hero standing next to them.

**Running gag — the third state.** It arrives at the worst possible moment and
nobody in frame ever acknowledges it. Escalation, in order: *mildly vacant →
visibly absent → fully unresponsive → a coat-shaped puddle on the floor while
the meeting continues over the top of them.*

**Style block:** the comedy mandate verbatim from `PRODUCTION-PLAYBOOK.md`, plus
the explicit final constraint: *no letters, words, numbers, labels, signage or
sound-effect text anywhere.* Never stage a prop on or behind the head.
