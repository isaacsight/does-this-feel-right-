Set in Palmier with `add_texts`, never `add_captions`. ASR mangles Turkheimer,
Norenzayan, Munafò and Kosmyna; every name below is typed from this file.

House type, house colours. On this film the palette is the world's: fluorescent
pink and deep blue on off-white stock. Type in the deep blue on bare paper — the
colophon is the one place in the film where lettering is allowed, because no image
model can draw it and the Editor owns every letterform.

## What the card must NOT do

- No "hosted by", no name, no face, no recurring identity. The narrator is never a
  character — it is the single thing keeping these films outside the rule that
  demonetises AI personas discussing health, and this film lands on psychiatry
  and IQ.
- Do not editorialise past the evidence. The Munafò line is deliberately hedged;
  keep the hedge.
- No emoji.
