# You Watched It Happen — frame book

133 frames across 7 acts. Written by a four-phase agent workflow on
2026-07-31 and gated mechanically: the script passes every voice target
(ease 81.1, grade 6.1, "you" 40.8/1k, "we" 12.5/1k) and **every frame was
boarded as a scene** — the workflow's own `is_scene` critic flagged zero
diagrams, which is the defect that cost thirteen frames on the last film.

Timings in [`production/beats.json`](production/beats.json), estimated 6:39.
Acts and paper in [`production/acts.json`](production/acts.json).

## The rule every frame here obeys

**A person doing a specific thing in a specific place.** Never a diagram of the
idea. When the LINE is abstract, that is exactly when the IMAGE must be
concrete — the narration already carries the abstraction and the picture's job
is to give it a body.

## Rules for every prompt

Flat mid-century comic line art. Cream paper, ink `#1F1E1D`, flat colour fills.
NO halftone, NO dot shading, NO texture. **Full bleed to all four edges** — no
painted margin, no inset panel, no border. **No lettering inside any image**;
all type is added at assembly. Paper colour is a per-act TINT applied at
assembly and is never prompted.

*the party, the house paper - the film opens where the viewer lives*
