# CLAIMS — The Million-Dollar Pigeon (pigeon racing)

Every factual claim available to the script, with sourcing status.
Drafted 2026-08-07. The writers may only build gags on VERIFIED rows;
anything else needs a check before it reaches the board.

## Verified (web, 2026-08-07)

1. **New Kim sold for €1.6M (~$1.9M), November 2020.** A two-year-old Belgian
   hen, bought by a Chinese buyer after two bidders operating under the
   pseudonyms "Super Duper" and "Hitman" drove the price up €280,000 in the
   final half hour of a two-week auction at PIPA. Broke the record set by
   Armando (€1.252M, 2019). Unusual twice over: a hen (cocks command more,
   for breeding), and she had raced only one season.
   - CBS News, NBC News, Reuters syndications, 2020-11-15.

2. **The 2013 Belgian doping scandal.** The Belgian federation sent samples
   from ~20 birds to a South African lab (the National Horseracing Authority):
   six positives — five for a human painkiller, one for COCAINE. Belgian tests
   had found nothing. Samples were anonymous, so nobody could be punished.
   - BBC, Time, road.cc, October 2013.

3. **Cher Ami and the Lost Battalion, October 1918.** With the trapped
   battalion under American artillery, the last pigeon flew ~25 miles through
   gunfire, wounded, and the message got through; ~194 men came back. Awarded
   the French Croix de Guerre. Now in the Smithsonian.
   - Smithsonian NMAH fact sheet (which also flags a century of myth-making
     around the details — the script must not over-claim the leg/eye specifics).

4. **32 pigeons hold the Dickin Medal** — the animals' Victoria Cross,
   awarded 1943–1949: 32 pigeons, 18 dogs, 3 horses and one ship's cat.
   Pigeons are the most decorated animal in the history of the medal.
   - PDSA, Wikipedia (Dickin Medal).

5. **Darwin opens On the Origin of Species with pigeons.** Chapter One:
   variation under domestication, built on his own loft. He joined two London
   pigeon clubs — the Columbarian societies, split by class, gentlemen's clubs
   and working men's clubs, and he attended both. Artificial selection in
   pigeon breeding is the on-ramp to natural selection.
   - Origin of Species ch.1; Christ's College Darwin project; Cornell Living Bird.

6. **How a race actually works.** Birds are basketed at the club, driven to a
   release point (races 60–1,200 km), liberated together, and race to their
   OWN lofts. Scoring is velocity — distance over time, yards per minute —
   because every bird flies a different race. Timing was a sealed clock and a
   secret-numbered rubber ring; now RFID.
   - RPRA, club explainers, Wikipedia (pigeon racing).

7. **The widowhood method.** The standard motivation system: pair the birds,
   let the bond form, then separate them and race the cock — he flies home fast
   because his mate is there. The sport's entire engine is a bird that wants
   to get home to his wife.
   - RPRA-adjacent training literature, multiple fancier sources.

8. **Mike Tyson's first fight was over a pigeon.** A bully tore the head off
   one of his birds; Tyson, on record: "My first fight was with Gary the
   Bully… it was love at first fight." He has said "the first thing I ever
   loved in my life was a pigeon," kept lofts all his life, and fronted a
   racing show (Taking on Tyson, 2011).
   - Tyson's own X post; CBS News; SI.

9. **The bullet-train fraud, Shanghai 2018 — VERIFIED, promoted from
   quarantine.** Two racers surnamed Gong and Zhang entered the Shanghai
   Racing Pigeon Association's annual race, ~600 km from Henan to Shanghai.
   They had spent A YEAR preparing: raising the birds at TWO feeding points,
   one in Henan and one in Shanghai, so the pigeons treated both as home.
   On race day they let the birds fly to a spot near the start, packed them
   into MILK CARTONS, and carried them to Shanghai on a high-speed train.
   They took the top four prizes, worth over 1 million yuan (~$150,000).
   They were caught because the birds arrived TOO FAST — the times shattered
   the race record. They killed the birds and surrendered the prize money to
   cover it up. A Shanghai court gave both men three-year suspended sentences
   for fraud, and fined them 30,000 and 20,000 yuan.
   - South China Morning Post, CGTN, Inquirer, August 2018.

respectability spine. Every gag is a sourced fact.
