# CAST — Why Everything Takes Longer Than You Think

Counts are LAW at point of use. No character is a likeness of any real
person; Kahneman is named in narration but NEVER drawn; no committee
figure may resemble him; no architect is drawn.

## Principals

**THE MAN** — the recurring protagonist: a small slight man with a round face
and a big confident nose, his head bald and smooth except for a single lonely
PAIR of hairs at the very top of his crown — one thin hair kinking left, one
leaning right — a flat VERMILION RED coat with THREE round buttons, thin
stick legs, ABSURDLY oversized dark ink-blue shoes. WANT: the shelf done by
Sunday. His recurring face is BRIGHT MORNING CONFIDENCE — hopeful, lit,
utterly sincere — and it is never mocked; the film's arc bends it to a
worn, warm patience by the end.

**THE PLANK MOUNTAIN** — the monument (see WORLD.md): ceiling-high planks
and closed boxes, ONE completely BLANK white instruction sheet on the
floor, ONE small brass screw beside it, ONE tiny silver screwdriver in the
Man's hand. Same staging on every return; shrinks only in the final act.

**THE SILENT EXPERT** — the committee act's counterpart: ONE older woman in
a sage GREEN cardigan at the end seat of the folding table, hands folded on
ONE closed plain folder, her face composed and level — the face of someone
who has seen seven of these and says nothing. She appears in at least TWO
committee frames and ONE archive-corridor frame (standing alone among the
boxed, unread books).

## Supporting

**THE STUDENT** — the 1994 act: ONE young woman at a desk with ONE lamp and
ONE tall stack of completely BLANK pages, sleeves pushed up, face resolute.
**THE OBSERVERS** — THREE figures at a glass pane watching the student's
desk from the next room, faces kind and unsurprised.
**THE COMMITTEE** — SEVEN figures at ONE folding table (the silent expert
at the end seat), each with ONE plain cup; every face individuated,
composed, bright with the fresh-start look.
**THE BUILDERS** — tiny scaffold figures on the harbour shell, ant-small
against the white ribs (deadpan scale — never individuated at distance).
**THE PLANT** — ONE potted plant on the garage workbench: sprout, then
leggy, then spilling over the bench edge — the film's clock.

## Interaction quota

- The garage staging recurs at least THREE times with the SAME window and
  bench: seasons change outside (snow, blossom, amber), the plant grows,
  the mountain holds. State the season and plant stage at point of use.
- The Man holds the tiny screwdriver UP in at least TWO frames (the
  sufficient answer); in the final act it lies flat on the finished shelf.
- The silent expert's knowing face is composed, never smug, never winking.
- Two-figure frames state "exactly TWO figures"; committee frames state
  "exactly SEVEN figures at the table."

## Palette / era

Vermilion = the Man. Sage = the silent expert + committee room. Mustard =
lamplight + autumn window. Ink blue = winter window, tunnel act, shoes.
Dusty orange = harbour dusk + blossom. Warm wood tones belong to the
mountain alone.
