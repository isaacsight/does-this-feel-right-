## Video direction

- palette system: Warm ivory `#FAF9F6` is the paper ground, ink `#1F1E1D` carries body and rules, tomato `#E24E1B` is the only accent, and coffee `#6B4E3D` is supporting metadata. Use the bold-poster frame system exactly: square geometry, ruled grids, large EB Garamond display, Courier Prime-style tracked metadata, no soft cards or generic AI gradients.
- motion grammar: Premium editorial motion with smooth long-tail settles. Every frame reveals one spoken cue at a time across the full duration—especially the back half—using only the cited deterministic rules. No bounce, no infinite loops, no wall-clock or random motion. The harness transition is each frame’s exit.
- reveal model: Begin with only the phrase being spoken; bring later words, numbers, lines, or diagram layers on their matching narration cue. Primary content stays above the bottom 17% caption keep-out. A resolved frame holds still; subtle low-amplitude jitter is allowed only on one hero when the hold truly needs life.
- rhythm: Frames 1–3 build the idea, frames 4–6 use one continuous ruled meter-bench stage, Frame 7 is the calm thesis breather, and Frame 8 is the terminal lockup. Frame 7’s still read and Frame 8’s final hold are deliberate.
- negative list: No purple-blue futurism, holographic heads, fake OpenAI UI, particles, glassmorphism, rounded dashboard cards, screensaver drift, lazy breathing, back-half camera push, front-loaded slideshow entrances, or imagery that presents GPT 5.6 SOL as a released product.
