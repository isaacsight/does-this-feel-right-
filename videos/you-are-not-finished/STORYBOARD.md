# You Are Not Finished — frame book

87 frames across 34 scenes, 5:32, mean hold 3.82s. Timings in
[`production/beats.json`](production/beats.json), generated from the locked
[`script.txt`](script.txt) — never re-worded here.

## Cast

**THE PERSON** — the house character carried over from *Why Humans Need
Rituals*: bald, two hair tufts, red jacket over cream shirt, black trousers,
cream shoes, cream satchel on a strap. Channel continuity; the viewer should
recognise them.

**PAST SELVES** — the same character, fully drawn, distinguished only by period
signals: different jacket, different haircut, an obsolete object in hand. Never
labelled with dates in the image.

**THE FUTURE SELF** — a blank figure: the character's exact silhouette filled
flat cream, no face, no clothing detail, a thin ink outline. This is the film's
one recurring motif.

**THE COMPANION** — the cyan box robot, correct construction: flat cyan cube
head, two round black dot eyes, thin straight arms, two thin legs, no antenna,
no rivets, no visible joints. Appears as clerk, interviewer, statistician.

## Motif discipline

The blank figure is introduced (Act 1), diagnosed (Act 3), argued over
(Act 4), sold a ticket (Act 5) and steps forward filled-in (Act 6). It appears
in no frame where it is not doing work. No other metaphor recurs.

## Rules for every prompt

Flat mid-century comic line art. Cream paper `#F4E8C8`, ink `#1F1E1D`, red
jacket, cyan companion, olive-green foliage and props. Flat colour fills. NO halftone, NO dot shading, NO texture.
**Full bleed to all four edges — no painted margin, no inset panel, no
border.** No lettering inside any image; all type is added at assembly.
